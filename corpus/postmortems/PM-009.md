---
id: PM-009
title: Postgres primary lost quorum and failed over
date: '2025-10-30'
services: [postgres, orders, checkout]
---
The primary database node lost quorum after a network partition and the replica was promoted. Writes failed with connection refused for about three minutes until clients reconnected to the new primary.

A config release to orders had gone out two minutes earlier and was blamed at first.

How to tell: connection refused errors naming the old primary, a promotion event in the database logs, and recovery that matches the promotion time rather than any rollback.

Fix: shorter client reconnect backoff; documented the failover signature in the runbook.
