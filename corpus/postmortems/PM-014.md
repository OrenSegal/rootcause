id: PM-014
title: Internal certificate expired after silent renewal failures
date: '2026-02-02'
services: [cert-manager, ledger, payments]
---
The TLS certificate for an internal endpoint expired and every client call failed with x509 certificate has expired. Renewal had been failing for days: the DNS-01 challenge record was never found, and cert-manager gave up after its retries without paging anyone.

A payments config change earlier that day was the only recent release and was suspected first.

Root cause: infrastructure. The certificate renewal pipeline, not any service code.

Fix: renewed manually; alert on certificates under 14 days and on any failed renewal.
