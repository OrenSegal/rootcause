---
id: PM-020
title: Inactive replication slot filled the database volume
date: '2026-04-04'
services: [postgres, orders]
---
A logical replication slot for an analytics consumer went inactive. Postgres kept all write-ahead log since the slot's position, so the volume filled over two days. When it hit 100%, writes failed with No space left on device.

A release that added a slow reporting query landed earlier that day and was suspected, but it only made reads slower.

How to tell: volume usage climbing steadily, retained WAL growing, ENOSPC errors from the database.

Fix: dropped the stale slot, alert on retained WAL and on slot inactivity.
