---
id: PM-005
title: Search pods OOM-killed by an unbounded result cache
date: '2025-02-14'
services: [search]
---
Memory on search pods grew slowly over two days until containers hit their 4Gi limit and were OOM-killed in turn. While a pod restarted it was marked not ready and requests routed to it failed.

No deploy was involved. The growth came from a per-pod result cache with no size limit, which filled faster after a traffic change.

How to tell: a steady upward memory line, GC pauses getting longer before the kill, restarts counted on the same pods, error rate matching the share of pods restarting.

Fix: bounded the cache by entry count and bytes; memory alert at 85% of limit.
