---
id: PM-003
title: Card acquirer partial outage
date: '2025-06-19'
services: [card-gateway, payments]
---
Our card acquirer had a partial outage on card authorization. The card gateway logged 503 Service Unavailable from the acquirer for about 40 minutes, and payments surfaced those as 502s.

A payments deploy had gone out ten minutes earlier and the first responder rolled it back. Errors did not change, which cost us 15 minutes.

How to tell: the failing calls are the gateway's upstream calls, the gateway's upstream error rate jumps while its own latency is flat, and the acquirer status page confirms it.

Fix: failover to the secondary acquirer for authorizations, and an alert on upstream error rate per acquirer.
