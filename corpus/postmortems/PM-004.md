---
id: PM-004
title: Receipt template release broke confirmation emails
date: '2025-07-08'
services: [payments, notifications]
---
A payments release changed the receipt email template and a missing variable made the template render fail. Payments itself was unaffected: charges succeeded, only the confirmation emails failed.

Impact: 2,100 customers did not get a receipt. Resent within the day.

Root cause: template variable renamed in one place. Caught by a customer support ticket, not by monitoring.

Fix: render test for every template in CI.
