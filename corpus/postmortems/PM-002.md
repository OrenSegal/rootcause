---
id: PM-002
title: Checkout latency from payment capture retries
date: '2025-05-02'
services: [checkout, payments]
---
Checkout p99 rose to 1.8s after payments raised capture retries from 2 to 4. Each retry held the checkout request open.

Impact: slower checkouts for 50 minutes, no failed orders.

What we saw: checkout latency up, payments upstream retry counters up, checkout database pool normal.

Root cause: retry count in payments. Checkout was healthy; it was waiting on payments.

Fix: retries back to 2 with a total time budget. Lesson: when checkout is slow, check whether its own resources are saturated before blaming it.
