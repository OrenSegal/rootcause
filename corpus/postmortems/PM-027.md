---
id: PM-027
title: Tax engine returned no rate for a newly supported country
date: '2026-02-11'
services: [payments, checkout]
---
A payments release enabled a new tax engine for EU orders. For one newly added country the rate table had no entry and the code dereferenced it, so those payments failed and checkout reported 500s.

Another team deployed shipping in the same minute, and its deploy was the most recent one before the errors.

Fix: rolled back payments; the engine now fails closed with a clear error and the rate table is validated in CI.
