---
id: PM-016
title: Search-domain migration broke name resolution after TTL
date: '2026-03-18'
services: [service-registry, orders, shipping]
---
The platform team moved internal service names to a new DNS zone. Clients still used the old search domain from cache, so nothing broke until cached answers expired five minutes later. Then lookups for the new names returned no such host across several services.

An orders release landed between the registry change and the errors and was blamed first.

How to tell: NXDOMAIN rate on the registry jumps, errors say no such host, and several unrelated services fail at the same moment.

Fix: served both zones during migrations; changes to resolution now roll out with a TTL-aware plan.
