---
id: PM-030
title: Session format change failed once old sessions expired
date: '2026-04-15'
services: [cart]
---
A cart release changed how sessions are serialized. Existing sessions in the cache were in the old format. Nothing failed until those sessions started to expire and be re-read about five minutes later, after an unrelated release had landed.

Root cause: the cart release's code, which could not read the old format. Fix: rolled back with operator approval, then re-released with a reader for both formats.
