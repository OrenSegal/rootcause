---
id: PM-024
title: Client retry policy turned a short pause into an outage
date: '2026-03-05'
services: [web-bff, product-api, catalog]
---
The catalog database paused for about a minute during maintenance. That alone would have been a blip, but the frontend's backend had been configured weeks earlier to retry product calls five times with no backoff. Request rate to the product API went up six times and its worker pool saturated, and it stayed down well after catalog recovered.

A product API release that morning was blamed first.

Root cause: the retry configuration in the caller two hops away. Fix: retries back to one with jittered backoff, and a retry budget per client.
