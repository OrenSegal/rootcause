---
id: PM-007
title: False latency pages from a cold synthetic probe
date: '2025-04-22'
services: [synthetic-canary, checkout]
---
We were paged for checkout p99 above 2s. Only one evaluation window was high, and the only slow requests came from the synthetic probe in one region, on its first run after the probe runner was redeployed.

Real user traffic was normal: error rate flat, organic latency flat. A config change to log verbosity earlier that hour was not involved.

Fix: the probe now runs a warm-up request that is excluded from the alert, and the alert needs two consecutive windows. Classified as no incident.
