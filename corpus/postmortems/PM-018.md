---
id: PM-018
title: SMS provider daily quota exhausted during a campaign
date: '2025-11-29'
services: [sms-gateway, signup]
---
Signup verification codes stopped sending. The SMS gateway was getting 429 responses from the provider: the daily message quota was used up. A marketing campaign had raised signups, but the traffic itself was well within capacity; the quota was the limit.

Category: an external dependency's limit, surfaced through our gateway.

Fix: raised the contracted quota, alert at 80% of daily quota, fallback provider for verification codes.
