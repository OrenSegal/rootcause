---
id: PM-028
title: Paged during an approved maintenance window
date: '2025-12-20'
services: [warehouse-db, reporting]
---
Reporting dashboards errored for 20 minutes and paged on-call. The warehouse database was in an approved storage maintenance window, announced the day before in the change calendar, and dashboards were expected to be unavailable.

Classified as no incident. Fix: alerts are suppressed for services named in an approved change, and the on-call checklist starts with the change calendar.
