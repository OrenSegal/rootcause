---
id: PM-034
title: Search pods restarting after a slow memory leak in the ranking library
date: '2025-11-19'
services: [search]
---
Search pods began restarting every few minutes with the JVM reporting it was out of heap. The error rate climbed with the restarts, since each pod served errors until it was ready again.

Impact: 40 minutes of degraded search, error rate peaked at 15%.

What we saw: OutOfMemoryError in the searcher, pods cycling, heap climbing steadily over the hours before the first restart. No deploy of the search service that day.

Root cause: a leak in the ranking library we had upgraded the week before. It kept a reference to every scorer it built, so heap grew with query volume until the limit.

Fix: pinned the library to the previous version, then took the upstream fix. Added an alert on heap growth rate.
