---
id: PM-035
title: Heap limit cut in a pod right-sizing change starved the indexer
date: '2026-02-03'
services: [indexer, platform]
---
A platform change to right-size pods lowered the JVM heap for the indexer from 6 GB to 1.5 GB. The service came back up and failed on its first large batch with OutOfMemoryError, restarted, and did it again.

Impact: index updates stopped for 25 minutes; search served stale results.

What we saw: the error from the first batch after the restart, not a slow climb, and the heap maximum reported by the JVM dropping at the moment of the change. The config diff of the right-sizing release had the new value.

Root cause: the heap setting in the release, not the code. The signature is the failure from the first real work after the restart and a heap ceiling that dropped at the same time.

Fix: restored the heap, and made the sizing tool refuse to cut a JVM heap below what the service's own dashboards show it using.
