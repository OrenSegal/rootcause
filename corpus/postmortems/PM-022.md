---
id: PM-022
title: Clock drift on one node broke token validation
date: '2026-02-21'
services: [ntp, auth, api-gateway]
---
One node lost all of its NTP sources and its clock ran ahead by about 90 seconds. Tokens issued by the auth instance on that node carried an issued-at time in the future, and the gateway rejected them. About a quarter of requests got 401s, matching that instance's share of traffic.

A signing key rotation the same morning looked related, and the first responder started reverting it.

Root cause: infrastructure, the node's time sync. Fix: restored NTP sources, alert on clock offset, and the gateway now allows a small skew.
